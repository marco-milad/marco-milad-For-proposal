# Module: Scoring and the audit report (PDF)

**Call it when** the user wants an audit report: a full SEO audit, a technical audit, a UX audit, or a "site health" PDF for a prospect. It also supplies the scoring rules that every other module uses.

## Scoring rules (all modules)

1. **Health and coverage are separate numbers.**
   - Each check resolves to **pass / fail / not checked / n.a.**
   - **Health** = passes ÷ (passes + fails), weighted by severity.
   - **Coverage** = checked ÷ applicable.
   - "Not checked" lowers coverage, **never health**. It is never shown as 0 and never as a pass.
2. **Data-sufficiency gate.** A category with coverage below ~50% shows "**not assessed (insufficient data)**" instead of a number. The overall score is labelled "**provisional**" when overall coverage is below ~70%.
3. **Our rubric, stated as ours.** Weights are an agency heuristic, not a Google score. The methodology page prints them, and they must sum to 100.
4. **Show the checks behind every score.** A number without its list of checks doesn't go into client copy.
5. **Falsifiability.** Every recommendation states the evidence it rests on, what it depends on or unblocks, and "**how we'd know it didn't work**" (the metric and the window).
6. **No impact or forecast language** (SKILL.md ground rules). Priority = أ/ب/ج + an effort class (small/medium/large). No hours, no "expected uplift", no revenue tables.

### Default category weights (adjust to the client's goal, and print the final weights)

| Report | Categories (weight) |
|---|---|
| **SEO health** | Technical 22 · Content & E-E-A-T 23 · On-page 20 · Schema 10 · Performance/CWV 10 · AI-search readiness 10 · Images 5 |
| **Local** | GBP 25 · Reviews 20 · On-page local 20 · NAP & citations 15 · Schema 10 · Local links 10 |
| **UX** | Usability heuristics 30 · Accessibility 20 · IA & navigation 20 · Conversion path 20 · Distinctiveness 10 (also reported as its own grade) |
| **IA** | Organization 30 · Navigation 30 · Labeling 20 · Search 20 |

**Grades:** A ≥ 85 · B 70–84 · C 55–69 · D 40–54 · E < 40. Always show coverage next to the grade, e.g. "B (74) · coverage 82%".

## Report structure (Arabic-led, bilingual terms, fixed A4 pages)

1. **Cover:** client, site, date, "Site audit".
2. **Summary:**
   - the scorecard per category (grade + coverage);
   - the 3–5 most important findings;
   - quick wins.
3. **What works.** Always include it. It's honest, and it shows what the new site must not lose.
4. **One section per category.** For each finding:
   - the finding;
   - the evidence (a screenshot or a number from raw data);
   - why it may matter (conditional wording);
   - the fix;
   - the priority and effort;
   - how we'd know it failed.
5. **Action plan in three horizons:** now (blocking issues) · next (key templates) · later (polish, content, authority). No dates presented as commitments.
6. **What we did not check, and why** (mandatory), plus the data that would unlock it (the D/U numbers).
7. **Data sources and methodology.** For each source: what it measures and how fresh it is, e.g.:
   - Lighthouse = lab, emulated mobile, one run per page;
   - CrUX = 28-day rolling field data;
   - Search Console = a 2–3 day lag;
   - a crawl date;
   - the AI prompt sample date and engines.

   Also print the weights and the evidence labels' meaning, in plain words.
8. **Glossary:** English term → Arabic explanation.

## Build pipeline

- **Separate data from rendering.** Write `report-data.json` (scores, coverage, findings, actions, sources), then generate the HTML from it, using a `build.py` like the prototype's. Numbers then come from one place and can be re-verified.
- **The HTML is Arabic RTL, on fixed `.page` A4 sections.** Follow [../rtl-bidi-gotchas.md](../rtl-bidi-gotchas.md). For charts (score bars, coverage), use inline SVG generated by `build.py`. No chart libraries needed.
- **Pre-export checks:**
  1. `pagecheck.mjs`: every page fits.
  2. `scan_language.py`: no impact language, no leaks.
  3. **Number re-verification:** every number in the HTML traces to `report-data.json` or the raw data.
  4. **A self-review list:**
     - no empty images
     - no section with almost no text
     - no duplicate tables
     - no internal labels (V/O/Gate)
     - the table of contents only lists sections that exist
- **Export.** Run `export-pdf.mjs` (headless browser, tagged PDF, fonts embedded). Don't use ReportLab-style generators: they can't shape Arabic or handle bidi.
- **Optional.** An `.xlsx` or `.csv` of the findings for the client's team. If you build it, escape cells that start with `= + - @` (spreadsheet formula injection).

## Prospect "mini audit" (pre-sales)

When the user only wants a short audit to open a conversation:
- 4–6 pages: summary, what works, the 5 key findings with screenshots, what we'd check next with access;
- public data only, labelled as such;
- the same honesty rules.

It must not read as a sales pitch built on fear.
